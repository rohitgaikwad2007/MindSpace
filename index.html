Tips for a balanced, fulfilling life

🧘 Stress Management

Take 5-minute breaks

Step away from screens every hour. A short walk or stretch dramatically reduces cortisol levels and refreshes focus.

Progressive muscle relaxation

Tense and release each muscle group for 5 seconds to release physical tension stored throughout the body.

Limit news consumption

Set a specific time for news. Constant updates increase anxiety. Try 15–20 minutes maximum per day.

Talk to someone

Sharing worries with a trusted friend or counselor reduces their psychological weight significantly.

📚 Study Tips

Pomodoro Technique

Study 25 minutes, rest 5. After 4 cycles, take a longer 15–30 minute break. Proven to boost sustained focus.

Active recall over re-reading

Test yourself instead of re-reading notes. Retrieval practice is 2–3× more effective for long-term retention.

Teach what you learn

Explaining concepts out loud reveals gaps in understanding and deepens memory consolidation.

🌙 Sleep Tips

Consistent sleep schedule

Go to bed and wake at the same time daily, even weekends. This anchors your circadian rhythm powerfully.

No screens 1 hour before bed

Blue light suppresses melatonin. Try journaling, light stretching, or reading instead.

Keep your room cool and dark

The optimal sleep temperature is 15–19°C. Darkness signals your brain it's time to rest deeply.
